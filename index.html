ChorePG

A family chore app that keeps household data on your own device.

ChorePG helps a family turn everyday chores into a shared quest board. Kids see their own tasks, check off progress, earn XP, build streaks, protect hearts, and celebrate level-ups. Adults create tasks, approve completed work, adjust rewards, manage family members, and review an audit trail of what changed.

Built for a household, not a social network

  • Kid-friendly quest boards, with daily, weekly, and one-off quests
  • Adult approval workflow before rewards are paid out
  • Shared Open Tasks with fair per-player contribution credit
  • XP, levels, streaks, hearts, and respawn mechanics
  • Optional household avatar and celebration media, managed by an adult
  • No ads, no third-party tracking, no third-party analytics
  • No public profiles, no friend lists, nothing shared outside the household

Your data stays with you

Household information is stored locally in the app on your device. ChorePG has no account to create, no household directory to join, and no server that collects your family's activity. Deleting the app, or clearing its app data, removes the local ChorePG data from that device.

ChorePG is designed for use inside one household by a parent or adult caregiver.
