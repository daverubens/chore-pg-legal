Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-03.

ChorePG is a private family chore and rewards app. It is designed for a household to manage quests, approvals, XP, levels, streaks, hearts, avatars, and related game settings.

Information Stored In The App

ChorePG stores the household information needed to run the app, such as family name, player names or initials, adult/kid roles, optional kid birth year, task, contribution, and approval history, XP/level/streak/heart state, settings, audit entries, avatar choices, and locally saved game content.

Adults may optionally add avatar photos, celebration videos, and monster intro videos. Those files are stored in the app data area.

Information Sent Off Device

  • On iOS, device backup may copy local app data to iCloud or to a computer backup, according to the device backup settings. This is an operating-system backup; the app developer does not receive the backup.

Ads, Tracking, And Analytics

ChorePG does not include advertising SDKs, third-party tracking, or third-party analytics. ChorePG does not sell household data.

Children And Family Use

ChorePG is intended for use by a parent or adult caregiver with members of their own household. Adults control setup, task creation, approvals, and optional connection features. The app does not ask children to create public accounts or share information publicly.

Permissions And Device Features

Deleting Data

Deleting the app or clearing its app data removes the local ChorePG data from that device. Device backups are managed by the user through the device iCloud and backup settings.

Changes

If ChorePG changes its privacy practices, this policy should be updated before submitting the changed build to an app store.

Contact

Use the support contact listed in the App Store or Google Play listing for privacy questions about this app.
